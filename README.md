# TheAmnesicBrowser

TheAmnesicBrowser is a privacy-focused web browser designed for people who want to browse the internet with greater anonymity and reduce their exposure to mass surveillance and online tracking.

The project was created with a simple goal: give users more control over their privacy while browsing the web.

## Privacy by Design

TheAmnesicBrowser is designed around privacy and anonymity. The browser operates primarily from RAM, helping minimize persistent browsing traces on the local machine.

It is particularly intended for:

* People concerned about online privacy
* Users looking to reduce tracking and surveillance
* Journalists and researchers working in sensitive environments
* Activists and human-rights defenders
* Professionals operating in monitored or high-risk areas
* Anyone who wants a more privacy-oriented browsing experience

TheAmnesicBrowser is designed to improve privacy, but no browser can guarantee complete anonymity or protection against every form of surveillance.

## Supported Platforms

Currently, TheAmnesicBrowser is designed for:

* Windows 10 — x64
* Windows 11 — x64

## Search Engines

The browser is designed to provide access to multiple search engines, including:

* DuckDuckGo
* Startpage
* Google
* Qwant
* Yahoo

This allows users to choose the search engine that best fits their needs.

## Search Engine

TheAmnesicBrowser's own search engine is currently under development.

The goal is to eventually provide a dedicated search experience integrated directly into the project, with privacy as a core principle.

## Why TheAmnesicBrowser?

Modern browsing can expose users to extensive tracking, profiling, fingerprinting, and data collection.

TheAmnesicBrowser was created as an independent project to explore a different approach: privacy should be accessible to everyone, without requiring advanced technical knowledge.

The project aims to make privacy-focused browsing simpler while continuing to improve its security and anonymity features.

## Project Status

TheAmnesicBrowser is currently under active development.

Features, privacy mechanisms, and the integrated search engine may evolve as the project progresses.

## Disclaimer

TheAmnesicBrowser is a privacy project and does not guarantee absolute anonymity or protection from surveillance.

Users remain responsible for their own security practices, operating-system security, network environment, and the websites or services they access.
